# Code of Conduct

Be rigorous with ideas and respectful with people.

SeniorOS discussions may challenge architecture, evidence, benchmarks, abstractions and process aggressively. They may not target people with harassment, discrimination, threats or abuse.

When reviewing a contribution:

- criticize the behavior or design, not the contributor;
- ask for evidence when claims are measurable;
- distinguish preference from correctness;
- assume mistakes before malice;
- keep security reports private until coordinated disclosure is appropriate.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when these boundaries are ignored.
